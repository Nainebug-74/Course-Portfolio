My Story

Discovering a passion for technology and web development through my everyday work in the IT field.

I currently work as a Field IT and am an aspiring **Web Developer**, currently studying for my Bachelor's degree in Computer Applications at Texas Tech University. My foundation in technology began with an Associate's degree in Computer Science from Howard College, and I love finding creative solutions to complex technical issues.

Web development is more than just code to me—it is a digital canvas to capture and celebrate the milestones of our journeys. Building these projects has given me a brand new lens to appreciate the structural details of software and web interfaces.

Technology & The Power of Memory

A large driving force behind my portfolio work is the realization that websites can serve as beautiful spaces for curation. My recent projects allow me to delve into the cherished memories I have traveling across 3 states by motorcycle with my late husband.

For several years, I struggled to look at past photographs without losing myself in sadness. Through coding and building custom web spaces, I have discovered that I can look at the past, celebrate what was, and smile at the depth of those experiences without losing myself. Every frame of our stories matters, and building clean interfaces to showcase them is a privilege.
